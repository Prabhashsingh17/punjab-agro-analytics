# Punjab Agro Centre — Data & Business Analytics Report

## 1. Problem Statement

Punjab Agro Centre (Gopalganj, Bihar) agricultural equipment bechta hai. Company ke paas data tha lekin:
- Business performance clearly measure nahi ho pa rahi thi
- Customer segments samajh nahi aate the
- Kaun sa product profitable hai — pata nahi tha
- District-wise expansion strategy missing thi

**Goal:** Data Analytics aur Business Analytics se actionable business decisions lena.

---

## 2. Analytics Approach

### Data Analytics Pipeline
```
Data Collection → Data Cleaning → EDA → Statistical Analysis → Visualization
```

### Business Analytics Pipeline
```
KPI Scorecard → Customer Segmentation → Profit Analysis → Market Share → Recommendations
```

| Layer | Type | Output |
|-------|------|--------|
| Layer 1 | Descriptive Analytics | KPIs, trends, performance |
| Layer 2 | Diagnostic Analytics | Segments, margins, root causes |
| Layer 3 | Prescriptive Analytics | Business recommendations |

---

## 3. Data Overview

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Time Period | Jan 2023 — Dec 2024 |
| Transactions | 1,179 |
| Customers | 350 |
| Districts | 17 (Bihar) |
| Products | 6 equipment types |
| Total Revenue | ~₹16.99 Crore |

---

## 4. Data Analytics Findings

- Revenue trend monthly level par track kiya — seasonal peaks identify kiye
- 17 Bihar districts se data — geographic distribution clear hua
- Payment mode distribution — Cash 35%, UPI 30%, EMI 15%
- Equipment-wise unit sales aur revenue comparison

---

## 5. Business Analytics Findings

### Customer Segmentation (CLV-based)
| Segment | Profile |
|---------|---------|
| High Value | ₹3L+ lifetime spend — loyalty focus |
| Medium Value | ₹1L–3L — upsell opportunity |
| Low Value | <₹1L — volume segment |

### Profitability Analysis
- **Harvester** — highest revenue AND highest estimated margin
- **Rotavator** — high volume, steady margin
- **Tawa** — budget segment, lower margin but high turnover

### Market Share
- **Gopalganj** = 28.2% (home market)
- **Chapra, Samastipur, Muzaffarpur** — top expansion districts

### Business Growth
- YoY growth analysis by equipment
- Seasonal demand: Kharif Prep + Rabi Prep peaks

---

## 6. Prescriptive Recommendations

| # | Action | Expected Impact |
|---|--------|-----------------|
| 1 | High Value customer loyalty program | Repeat business +15% |
| 2 | Sales agents in top 3 outstation districts | Geographic reach +20% |
| 3 | EMI tie-up for Harvester | Premium sales +25% |
| 4 | Pre-season stock planning (Jun, Oct) | Zero stockout |
| 5 | Dealer network expansion | Bulk order growth |
